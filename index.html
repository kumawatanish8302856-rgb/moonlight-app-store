How to Install Downloaded Apps

Step-by-step guide for Android APK & Windows PC

For Android Devices (.APK)
  1. Download APK: Click the "Download APK" button on any app card.
  2. Open Notification: When the download completes, tap the download notification or go to your Files / Downloads folder.
  3. Allow Unknown Sources: If Android displays "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps", tap Settings and switch on "Allow from this source".
  4. Tap Install: Return to the prompt and tap Install. Once done, tap Open!
All apps on Moonlight Store are clean and virus-scanned. Safe to install!
For Windows PC (.EXE / .ZIP)
  1. Click Download to save the setup installer or ZIP package.
  2. Double-click the downloaded file in your Downloads folder.
  3. If Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and select "Run anyway".
